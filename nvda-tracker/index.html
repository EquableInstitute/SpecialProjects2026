About these figures
  • Prices. NVDA closing prices, not adjusted for dividends, starting 1/2/2026. Closes come from Google Finance and StockAnalysis.com, cross-checked against a second source. On trading days, the current day's row holds the Google Finance price recorded at about 10 am and 1 pm ET (which may be delayed up to 20 minutes) and is replaced by the closing price after 4 pm.
  • Holdings. Shares come from each fund's own disclosures: SEC Form 13F filings for most funds, and published holdings listings, holdings reports, or annual comprehensive financial reports (ACFRs) for the rest. Each disclosure's share count applies from its as-of date until the next disclosure.
  • National estimate. Takes the sample funds' combined NVDA value as a share of their combined total assets and applies that share to the $6.4 trillion held by all U.S. state and local pension funds. All four figures are scaled by the same factor. This assumes funds outside the sample hold NVDA in the same proportion to assets as the sample funds.
  • Change since 11/6/2026. 11/6/2026 is the last close before NVIDIA's board of directors announced, on 11/8/2026, an investigation involving CEO Jensen Huang.
  • Holdings are a floor. 13F filings cover directly held and internally managed positions. Shares held through commingled funds or some external managers may not appear. Trades inside a quarter are not visible.
  • * Dated holdings. New York City (five funds combined), Georgia TRS, Massachusetts PRIM, Illinois TRS, and the WSIB Holdings Report last reported holdings as of 6/30/2025. Their share counts are held constant through the whole period. For MA PRIM, Illinois TRS and the WSIB Holdings Report, shares are derived from reported dollar values divided by the 6/30/2025 NVDA close ($157.99).
  • Coverage. The list covers the 25 largest U.S. public pension funds. North Carolina and UC Regents are shown as undisclosed and are not included in the totals. WSIB is represented by its holdings report; its 13F, which lists no NVDA position, is not shown.

Source: Equable Institute analysis of fund disclosures (from the 10/23/2026 AI Exposure workbook) and NVDA closing prices from StockAnalysis.com.
